Adaptive formation and positioning for drone swarms

Honours research · Supervisor: Prof. Athman Bouguettaya

I study energy-aware formation and positioning for swarm-based drone delivery in skyway networks, with the aim of maintaining delivery efficiency under changing wind and charging-resource constraints.

  • Conduct real-world DJI Tello experiments across front, column, vee, echelon, and diamond formations, recording battery levels, flight time, yaw, and position.
  • Design hover baselines and battery-quality checks, and analyze how formation, position, and wind affect energy consumption to inform adaptive decisions.

HyperTrace: graph learning for coordinated behavior detection

Collaborative research · Supervisor: Senior Engineer Xiaoliang Gong

I contributed the detection model and data and evaluation pipelines to HyperTrace, an evidence-centered review system for suspected coordinated information operations on social media.

  • Designed and trained Lorentz-HGT, combining a heterogeneous graph transformer with Lorentz hyperbolic representations.
  • Built consistent graph inputs from simulated and public data, and ran leave-one-scenario-out evaluations across five scenarios with three random seeds.

Self-Harness for language-model agents

Summer research internship · Supervisor: Prof. Guojun Qi

I reproduced the Self-Harness workflow using GLM-5, benchmarking Deep Agents on Terminal-Bench 2.0 through Harbor. The work examined how an agent can improve the harness through which it operates.

  • Conducted five rounds of candidate generation, benchmarking, and selection using held-in task performance.
  • Built automated evaluation pipelines and investigated agent, API, and verifier timeouts to stabilize the experiments.
